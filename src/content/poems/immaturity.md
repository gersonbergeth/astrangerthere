So let's hear it for immaturity 

Let's hear it for grown-ups that never grew up 
Nasty fiends who stare and say "I don't give a fuck" 

Those children with dreams of going to the sun 
Who are terribly afraid of dying before kingdom come 

Who fear no one but the ghost of stability
And refuse to live in the kingdom of piety 

Who won't bow to the Empress of good manner
And will always raise the pirate banner

Let's cheer to the health of animals at late noon 
Changelings that defy the light and cry at the moon

Hear hear to the woman that will drive you insane
May she always be a beautiful poisoned game

Hear hear to the man that will cut, strip and destroy
May he remain as a god in a forest of endless joy 

Long live short-fuse candles that burn to extinction 
Love, wealth and beauty to defiers of natural selection 

Raise half-empty cups to the sons of unbridled impulses 
Sing a loud song to the daughters of shameless caprices 

Immaturity to you, immaturity to me, immaturity to us
