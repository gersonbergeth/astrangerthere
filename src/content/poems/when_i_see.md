---
title: "When I First See Her"
author: "Stranger"
date: "01/04/2008"
location: "Algiers"
published: true
---
When I first see her, what should I say?
After many days and time differences
What can I say as soon as she takes me into her arms?
Passion, admiration maybe compassion 

It will be have to be impressive, that is certain
Not some empty remark that goes unnoticed 
It must make and impact, strike to the very core
She must remember that day, that temperature

I will stand there dressed as I can best afford
The flowers in my hand stiff and smelly 
My face shaved to baby-clean youth 
My teeth as white as they should always be

She will be tired from the altitude and the clouds
Looking as best as she can with fresh mascara
Worm clothes, mangled transcontinental hair
Stunning as when we swam in the south sea

What should I say? “I love you darling and missed you”
That would do in most meetings but not this one
It has been too long since I was warmed by her 
Since I was lifted from my pit to her tower

I could say nothing, pretend that my eyes speak 
Or wait for her to take the lead, “So do I, really” 
But if she stands, waiting for me to advance?
If I fail she will forgive, she always does, but I won’t fail 

So I will say, “It has not been the same without you”
And “I have been waiting for this moment” 
And “You make me complete my dear sweet”
All this I could say, but none are quite true, all is false 

I am here now as the multitude walks briskly 
Behind this fellow … one more stranger
Still the words escape me and I am running out of time
A simple deep kiss might do, this is magic that I know

There she is looking at my stupid face. Say something!
In a moment she will be in my personal space 
The panic of ignorance sets in and takes hold
The smile fades and the swagger slacks

The thrusting red roses speak for me 
I look like a fool, her previous lover was better than me
He would know what to say or at least would rehearse 
I am beaten and must find a way to retreat 

She takes the bouquet and smells the summer
Drops her proud shoulders and flashes a smile 
Her petal eyes blink in the sun and breeze
Her fingers take mine and we merge incomprehensibly

“So did I, my love” my dumb ears clearly 
A moment later I am with her without my trappings 
On cold inviting sheets expecting her attention 
What did I say? It must’ve been good or she is better than me
