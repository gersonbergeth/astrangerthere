---
title: "Kill the Bird"
author: "Stranger"
date: "03/07/2026"
location: "Kyiv"
published: true
---
Kill the bird, said the hunter 
It scares my prey, it flutters 
High above is too high for me 
My weapon needs silence to be 

Kill the bird, commanded the priest 
It inspires my pupil, it soars 
Dreams above is a sin to me 
Sermons need eyes that cannot see 

Kill the bird, whispered the lover 
It promises a better life, it tempts 
Love from above is too far for me 
My desire needs to be in exclusivity 

Kill the bird, cried the mother 
It robs me of the babe, it weeps 
Perched in the tower it must see 
That my bosom exposed is not free 

Kill the bird, exclaimed the painter 
It improves the canvas, it strokes 
Mine is vision and creation 
I am the creator not he or she 

Kill the bird, sang the birds 
It takes up all the space, it races
High in the dark blue damage be 
We are flock, a murmuration, a single company 

Kill the bird proclaimed the saviour 
It blesses all things, it coos 
It is I that returns in the clouds loudly 
It is I that carried the bloody cross proudly 

Kill me? Why me? asked the bird 
I don’t know any of you from my flights
Last I saw you, you did not see my span 
My wing tips tilted but you did not motion

Is this what you want?
To see the end of me?

The bird dove hard to the ground at speed 
Wings pulled back into diamond shape
A bed of rocks seemed to be its final spot 
All watched and screamed: die, kill, bird, die, kill, bird 

Yet in the last millisecond the bird lifted its head 
And elegantly, the rest of the carriage followed 
It planed above water and inched over waves
As it banked to the left its beak looked back and said 

I never die and you cannot kill me 
I alone decide my lonely destiny 
Kill yourselves if you must 
I only in the loving wind trust
