For the good of the game 
He sold all that he had
For the good of the game
He said goodbye and never looked back 

For the good of the moment
He rephrased the words 
For the good of the moment 
She understood what hurts 

For the good of the nation 
The leaders build high walls
For the good of the nation 
They dug tunnels into foreign halls 

For the good of the tune 
Choice words were deleted
For the good of the tune 
A beckoning voice was depleted 

For the good of a lie 
The more it was told 
For the good of a lie 
Doubters were made cold 

For the good of our life
A new order was put in place 
For the good of our life 
What is ‘life’ is soon replaced

For the good of the truth 
I’ll stand with the few 
For the good of the truth 
We will die through and through 

For the good of love 
Our hearts will break 
For the good of love 
I’ll give all that you can take

For the good of your sanity 
I’ll stop and not say another word
