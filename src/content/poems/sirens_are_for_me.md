---
title: "These Sirens Are For Me"
author: "Stranger"
date: "01/06/2003"
location: "Jakarta"
published: true
---
I hear the far-away wailing 
Down the street to the rescue 
A mental note of danger 
I’m not worried as an observer
The red lights pass me over 

Death and pain they herald 
Proudly they remind me 
All is not well in paradise 
Hell has come to some door 
All is changed not like yesterday 

Drown the ugly with white sheets 
Sensor the repulsive with blur   
My reality is here with you 
Inside away from the outside 
We are strong in our safety 

Twisting metal and steel 
Woven into body and skin
A sudden impulse 
a mechanical failure 
The work of destiny’s tailor

Flesh and bones are strong 
yet in the modern jungle 
Beasts are brutal and remorseless
Gashing and cutting through
Without a sense of justice 

Why a child crossing the road?
Why a man on a scaffold? 
Why a girl walking in tall grass? 
Why a boy playing swords?
No reason just hard cold facts 

The sirens run on through the night
Do I know any of the unlucky?
Maybe yes but most likely no 
It could never happen to close 
Only they suffer not we, not me 

Justice is selective in all things
It selects who will live or die
Who will walk tall or cry 
Rise every day or lie till the end
Justice is playing a game 

So as I trust in justice still 
I watch the pain displayed 
Hard I am and yet caring 
Not knowing if justice will 
Listen to sane arguments 

It’s all the same to me 
As long at those sirens 
Bear inconsequential news
