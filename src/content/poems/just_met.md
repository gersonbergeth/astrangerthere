title: "Just Met"
author: "Stranger"
date: "01/02/2018"
location: "Bangkok"
published: true
---
Her apartment was so small
A narrow bed, a round coffee table and a pile of clothes 
Her apartment was so small 
I walked in and crossed to the other end in less than half a second 
Her apartment was so small 

Her mind was so big 
A PhD, several published articles and temperance 
Her mind was so big 
I asked and she answered and answered and answered  
Her mind was so big 

Her life was so short 
At the middle of 30’s close to maturity but not there 
Her life was so short 
Just arrived in the world by way of Phoenix, London and Bangkok
Her life was so short 

Her wisdom was so long 
Had friends in prisons, in castles and really big institutions 
Her wisdom was so long 
She could tell if I half-lied, full-lied or just memorized 
Her wisdom was so long 

Her eyes were so sad 
Been hurt many times by others but not by me 
Her eyes were so sad 
“I cry often”,  she said, “but I now, I schedule my tears” 
Her eyes were so sad 

Her teeth were so white 
Like piano keys or plastic dominos or clean sheets 
Her teeth were so white 
“Doctor said I have a perfect smile”, she said, and doctor was right 
Her teeth were so white 

I could not tell if she was poor or rich, if she was here or there, if she was interested or just passing time

Did she want a guy like me, or like someone else, was she staying, and most urgently: 

Would she see me again?  

All I did know was this:

Her apartment was so small, but her mind so big 
Her life was so short, but her wisdom so long 
Her eyes were so sad, but her teeth so white
